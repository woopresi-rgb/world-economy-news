N News  ·  C Commentary, column, editorial or analysis (opinion)

    How stories are chosen and how to read them
    • Each region has two pieces: one news story, with government and central-bank policy announcements first, and one editorial, column or commentary on it. If no policy announcement is available, the most important economic story is used.
    • Regions are assigned by what the story is about, not where the outlet is based.
    • Preferred sources: WSJ, NYT, Financial Times, The Economist, Nihon Keizai Shimbun, Nikkei Asia, The Globe and Mail, Financial Post.
    • For paywalled stories, we use reliable wire services or business papers that explicitly cite the original, or authorized republications, and list the original source and the provider separately.
    • Same-day stories (Korea time) come first; major stories from the past seven days are also allowed.
    • Headlines are shown in English; a headline translated from another language is marked “Translated”.
    • The news summary covers what the article reports; “Outlook” is a forecast or analysis in it. Editorials, columns and commentary are opinion and are labelled as such.
    • ★ marks a paywalled link; links to free republications carry no star.
    • Excluded: simple stock-price moves, stock picks, promotional pieces, and repeat coverage of the same event.
